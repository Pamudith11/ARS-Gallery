FAQs

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your session with us.

For weddings, we recommend booking 2–4 months in advance as dates fill up quickly. For portrait and event sessions, 2–4 weeks notice is usually sufficient. Contact us early to secure your preferred date!

We cover a wide area for sessions and events. Travel costs may apply for distant locations. Contact us to discuss your specific needs and we'll find the best solution.

Wedding highlights are delivered within 1 week. Full edited wedding galleries within 3–5 weeks. Portrait and event sessions within 1–2 weeks. All photos are delivered via a private online gallery link.

A deposit is required to confirm and secure your booking date. The remaining balance is due on the shoot day. We accept bank transfers and cash payments. Contact us for a detailed quote.

Absolutely! We love when clients share mood boards, Pinterest boards, or specific ideas before the session. We'll incorporate your vision while adding our own creative touch for something truly unique.

For outdoor portrait sessions, we can reschedule at no extra charge if the weather is unsuitable. For weddings and events, we always have a backup plan and the skill to create beautiful results in any conditions.
